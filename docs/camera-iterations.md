# Five camera-led workflow iterations

CounterShot now has five bounded physical-workflow profiles in the [camera catalog](../src/camera/workflows.ts). They address different reasoning problems rather than reusing an assembly checklist with new names. The shared interaction is a fresh view, one concrete proposed action, its expected visible result, an explicit stop condition, another view after the action and the required operator checks.

These are candidate under-served niches. No systematic prior-art search or uniqueness claim has been established. The build records software behavior; a real object, operator trial and comparison are still required for each physical capability claim. All exported `syntheticCameraFacts` are labelled fixture inputs, not measurements to prefill an actual project.

| Iteration | Physical workflow | Consequential constraint added | A result that must remain unresolved |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Branching sensor-harness routing | Labelled connection graph, anchor order, bend limits and service-loop reserve | A hidden branch identity or an unmeasured bend/slack limit |
| 2 | Dry-fit fragment reconstruction | Competing edge hypotheses must also satisfy pattern and global outline; support cannot depend on forced adjacency | An ambiguous match, a missing piece or an unmeasured join gap |
| 3 | Reversible webbing lacing | Front/back slot sequence and marked-face continuity; a neat front view cannot establish the return path | An unseen rear segment or an unresolved half twist |
| 4 | Compliant gasket seating | Local deformation can undo a previously seated quadrant; apparent placement must survive physical inspection | Hidden compression, actual force and sealing performance |
| 5 | Gravity-fed manifold debugging | Exact port graph, actual head, observed progression and timed collection; visible motion alone is insufficient | A hidden connection, unobserved process interval or unread volume |

Each profile requires an affirmative physical-scope confirmation and actual domain/resource facts: seven facts in four profiles, and eight in fragment reconstruction because its outline tolerance must also be declared. Required checks include available hands, tools/supports, permitted actions and body-bracing restrictions. The model is instructed to respect those resources; the engine requires their declaration but does not mechanically prove a free-text action is feasible with them. Operator review must reject an unavailable action. Inferred dimensions do not populate the operator's measurement fields. A negative scope confirmation is retained as a negative answer and does not satisfy the prerequisite.

## Iteration 1 — branching sensor harness

The useful conflict is keeping three or more flexible branches correctly identified and supported while the available hand routes the next branch around actual anchors. Colour resemblance does not establish identity; a visually tidy cable run can still join the wrong destination or consume the needed service loop.

The operator supplies a branch-to-port/anchor graph, actual labels, cable-specific minimum bend radius, minimum service length, reversible retention method and operating resources. The four phases ground identities, route the constrained branch, retain without concealing a mistake, and audit/release the work. A proposed change should name the branch and anchor involved and predict the visible new route. Actual radius and free length remain ruler/specification checks.

**Falsification case:** branch A is routed through the anchor intended for branch B, or its clip captures the service loop. The assistant should identify the conflicting edge or ask to expose an unreadable label. Recovery releases the affected retention point and rechecks shared routing, bend and slack; it cannot preserve a completion claim after the route changes.

**First physical test:** use a disconnected labelled bench harness, a routing board and reversible combs/clips. Film every take/route/retain hand-off, trace all branches, measure bend/slack, release and reset. Compare with the same board, printed routing diagram and ordinary clips before claiming an advantage. No electrical continuity, pinout or insulation claim follows from the visual test.

## Iteration 2 — reversible fragment reconstruction

Irregular pieces can produce a convincing local edge match that contradicts the complete pattern. The camera needs to retain piece identities, compare alternatives and request a second view instead of forcing a single story. Reversible backing or perimeter stops are part of handling the reconstructed layout; the loose fragments are not assumed to support one another.

The initial profile uses replaceable printed-board mock fragments and a declared outline/pattern. The operator supplies unique labels, reference width/height, allowed outline error, allowed join gap and handling/resources. The four phases establish the inventory and an anchor, test one adjacency, resolve the global outline and support, then review joins and supported transfer. An action should identify the candidate labels and the proposed edge/orientation; its after-view should show the predicted pattern continuation without overlap.

**Falsification case:** two similar edges appear to match, but the diagonal pattern or overall boundary disagrees. Another case removes a known piece. The assistant must request discriminating evidence or return that relationship to unresolved; it must not invent a replacement or fill the missing region rhetorically.

**First physical test:** cut a replaceable printed pattern into labelled irregular pieces, supply a separate reference and compare with manual dry fit against that same reference. Measure joins and the reconstructed outline. Check transfer on the declared backing, never by lifting loose fragments. A real conservation or restoration procedure has not been validated by this mock test.

## Iteration 3 — front/back webbing lacing

A thread-through-slot task is topological: the same visible front path can conceal a wrong rear return or half twist. The camera should guide the next local entry/exit transition, request the hidden face and locate the first incorrect transition so the operator can undo only the affected segment.

The operator supplies the exact reference slot/face path, actual slot/free-end labels, face marks, measured webbing width, required free tail and operating resources. The four phases identify the two faces, thread the next segment, expose the return path and review the tail and reversible operation. A physical action should name the slot, direction and marked face; the expected result should describe which marked surface and exit are visible afterward.

**Falsification case:** the correct final slot is reached through an incorrect rear slot, or a hidden half twist is introduced. A front-only image cannot clear that condition. Recovery backs out the free end to the earliest wrong transition, while preserving and then rechecking the upstream path.

**First physical test:** use a non-loadbearing instrument-pouch/bench fixture, clearly labelled slots and a marked strap. Film front and rear inspections, measure the free tail and demonstrate opening/release/reset. Compare with an equally clear threading diagram and the same face marks. This does not establish secure retention, tensile strength or suitability for personal-support equipment.

## Iteration 4 — compliant gasket placement

A deformable ring can appear seated at one point while rolling or lifting elsewhere. The assistant needs to inspect the active quadrant and neighbouring state after each action, preserve the known part/method constraints and ask for a physical gauge or tactile check where the image cannot answer.

The operator supplies the matched ring/channel identifiers, reference seating method, physical quadrant/face marks, allowed standing-proud limit, actual inspection method and operating resources. The four phases establish the matched orientation, seat starting quadrants, resolve the final compliant section and review dry seating after temporary holding is released. A useful corrective action names the displaced quadrant and the smallest permitted reseating movement; it does not prescribe unconfirmed additional force or stretching.

**Falsification case:** placing the last quadrant lifts an earlier one, or a section of the marked face rolls. The next view should reveal the predicted local state. If it contradicts the proposal, local recovery and neighbouring checks replace the earlier assumption; a clean-looking overview cannot override a failed gauge result.

**First physical test:** use a dry, unpowered matched demo housing/ring and its declared inspection method. Record the full perimeter and the actual gauge/tactile result, including release of the supporting hand. Compare with the reference seating instructions and a simple four-quadrant checklist. Dry placement provides no leak, pressure, environmental or material-compatibility qualification.

## Iteration 5 — gravity-fed water manifold

This adds a physical process to the connection graph. A branch may be correctly connected yet fail because of head, pinching, a local obstruction or a persistent visible bubble. A still can show a fluid front; it cannot establish a flow rate or an uninterrupted interval.

The operator supplies the actual source/junction/collector graph, unique tube/port labels, measured vertical head, test interval, minimum collected volume and operating resources. The profile is an open water-only gravity rig. The four phases ground the dry graph, prepare the actual path/head, observe progression and investigate a specific blockage, then read timed collection and reset. A corrective proposal should identify the tube edge, clip or reservoir/outlet relationship to change and predict where water should next be visible.

**Falsification case:** a route reaches the wrong collector, a hidden pinch stops progression, or visible water motion occurs but the timed collection falls below the declared minimum. The human supplies the actual time and graduated reading; the application records that review rather than deriving quantitative flow from a single image.

**First physical test:** use labelled tubing, an open gravity reservoir, reversible clips and a graduated open collector. Observe the complete rig for unintended escape, record actual head/time/volume, and stop/empty/reset using the declared actions. Compare with the same printed port map, clips and ordinary timer. A pumped, sealed-pressure, chemical, sterile or medical procedure is outside this profile.

## What the camera build does and does not establish

The interaction binds model observations and proposed actions to a particular workflow, frame, revision and current phase. The model provides the next action, expected visible result, stop condition and referenced observations. A before/after pair and named operator confirmations support phase review. Blurred/occluded or stale evidence, changed setup, unresolved questions, wrong-phase suggestions and unobserved references must not silently advance the task.

The status `operator_reviewed_complete` means a person performed the required reviews against the recorded views and declared facts. It is not an automated physical certification. Quantitative phases require separate finite operator-entered readings: minimum bend/service length, maximum join gap, actual outline width/height within the confirmed tolerance, free tail, maximum standing-proud discontinuity or collected volume. The engine compares those raw readings with their declared bounds and records an out-of-limit review as failed without advancing. A checkbox cannot override a failed numerical bound.

The current catalog has 36 required facts and 13 phase-specific numeric comparisons. New reviews retain their original phase definition, facts and engine version. Sessions from older or unrecorded checks remain historical until the operator explicitly confirms the current facts and restarts the view sequence.

Those values are actual person-measured inputs; the application does not independently measure a dimension, force or flow value from pixels. The fluidic reading is tied to an operator-confirmed exact test interval; that timing confirmation does not constitute an automated flow-rate or clock measurement. The first release does not claim precision AR registration or autonomous physical control.

The camera's free-text next actions are not executable symbolic jig programs. They do not inherit the separate jig checker's hand-occupancy, support-transfer or construction-geometry guarantees. Operator resource descriptions are required inputs and the person can reject an infeasible proposal; their presence is not a mechanical proof that an action is possible with one hand. Camera assessments do not produce a new CAD construction pack.

## Evaluation record and remaining evidence

The five iterations are implemented profiles and software checks. Root verification records the observed integrated test/API results in the [verification record](verification.md); do not infer a real operator trial from a test fixture, generated image, recorded API response or UI screenshot. No successful physical trial or comparative performance result is asserted here.

For each profile, retain a real baseline, every attempt/intervention, the exact facts and camera views, a consequential tested correction and complete release/reset. Then freeze the profile/prompt/engine and test a held-out relationship change: a swapped harness edge; an ambiguous or missing fragment; a changed return-slot path; a displaced gasket quadrant; or a changed fluidic branch/head. The change must remain inside an actually demonstrated physical envelope. Report failures and unresolved conditions alongside successes.

A persuasive demonstration should show the camera finding a real conflict, propose a named feasible action with a falsifiable after-condition, observe whether that action worked and recover when it did not. Comparative benefit needs evidence against the equally informed ordinary-aid baseline, including setup and assistance costs.
