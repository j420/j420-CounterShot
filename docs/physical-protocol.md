# Physical admission and complete-cycle protocol

This protocol establishes what was observed for a named prototype. It does not establish clinical suitability, universal accessibility, fatigue reduction or transport protection.

## Declare the case

Record the actual contents, package subtype, materials, thickness, closure, available hand/actions and allowed body support. If a person voluntarily uses one hand for an engineering demonstration, describe it that way. Do not present that demonstration as validation with a person who has a disability.

Start with one measured wraparound package or a precisely described top-opening tuck carton. Record opening face, flap/closure arrangement and allowed orientation; “any carton” is unsupported. Record the expected contents and the limited handling/closure inspection that determines success.

## Calibrate the material

1. Measure the item/bundle, usable carton interior, sheet and board thickness. Keep units explicit.
2. Cut a small fit coupon using the actual board and tooling. Measure a usable slot/fit allowance rather than relying on model-generated values.
3. Test the intended fold/score and record its allowance and springback. Identify grain or flute direction if it changes the result.
4. Verify printed dimensions with the drawing's scale square. Never silently shrink a drawing to fit a page. Use measured ruler marking or a tested tiled print if necessary.
5. Record each substitution or correction. A material or geometry change requires new checks and physical trials; an assembly error corrected to the same design is recorded separately.

Design choices such as stop height may be bounded proposed variables. They must not be mislabelled as measured material properties. A passing fit coupon does not establish every later composition's stability.

## Admit the mechanism

Test the whole operation using the actual blank and materials:

| Check               | What to observe                                                                          |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Fresh supply        | Operator takes an unused package from the agreed supply; no hidden helper preload        |
| Loading             | Initial flat blank or open carton enters the station with all necessary clearance        |
| Support transfer    | An object remains supported when the permitted hand leaves to perform the next operation |
| Folding and closure | Full flap travel and closure remain possible; no rail blocks the blank                   |
| Extraction          | No clip, peg or other permanent support becomes trapped inside the parcel                |
| Reset               | Station returns to a state where the next fresh package can be used independently        |

A snug nest may fit the finished box while blocking the unfolded blank. Test both. Sliding a flexible bag over a docked insert is a different mechanism and requires its own travel-path test; do not assume the wrap or carton result covers it.

The shared insert must beat or enable something beyond a simple pre-creased wrapper/holder. If it adds construction, material and handling without a useful benefit, remove it.

## Record complete trials

The cycle starts when the operator reaches for unused packaging and ends when the finished parcel is released and the station is ready for another order. Include labels only when they are part of the declared job. Initial maker assistance is permitted when disclosed; it does not permit operating assistance under a goal of independent one-hand operation.

Record an uninterrupted view, design/input identifiers, actual materials, date, operator and reviewer roles, contents, closure, release, reset, allowed-resource compliance, assistance, errors and duration. A human inspects criterion results. Occlusion or missing footage is incomplete evidence, not automatically a pass or a physical failure.

Early admission target: three consecutive complete cycles. Proposed demonstration targets: five consecutive primary-condition cycles and three after adaptation, while retaining **all** attempts. These small samples do not demonstrate general reliability. Keep construction time separate from operating time.

Stop when the operator wishes. Do not infer force, fatigue or a person's physical ability from camera footage. A genuinely blocked original attempt is valid input; supply the intended remaining work through a labelled helper demonstration or description.

## Revise from evidence

Describe what actually failed and the contradicted assumption. Ask for a targeted measurement or trial when the cause is uncertain. Bind the revision, drawings, material/build record and new evidence together. A wider part is a dimensional revision; a structural adaptation changes support responsibilities or operation dependencies as well.
