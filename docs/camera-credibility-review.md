# Independent credibility review: five camera-led workflows

This review evaluates the new camera-led direction against the supplied implementation specification, the user's later scope changes and the supplied hackathon text. The five workflows are a credible exploration set because they require different kinds of physical reasoning. They have not been established as untouched research areas or as five physically successful solutions.

The original specification described packing and cited substantial prior work in fixtures, tool/action design and one-handed operation. The user's later request changes the supported activities. It does not provide evidence that a new activity works or that a camera can measure an unobserved physical property.

## What makes the five iterations distinct

| Iteration | Physical reasoning burden | Minimum consequential adaptation | Required evidence and uncertainty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Disconnected sensor-harness routing | Branch topology, service-loop placement, crossing order and access to labelled destinations. | Identify the exact branch and propose a different route or temporary support placement that resolves a declared obstruction while retaining the target map. | Before/after views must preserve branch identity. Actual bend/slack limits come from confirmed facts. Electrical continuity and hidden destinations cannot be inferred from a still. |
| Dry-fit fragment reconstruction | Irregular-piece identity, candidate adjacency, orientation and support during assembly. | Propose one labelled adjacency/rotation and a reversible support arrangement; distinguish a visually plausible edge from a confirmed fit. | Use labelled replicas or printed mock pieces and a declared target. Repeated texture and hidden edges should trigger another view. No adhesive, restoration or real-heritage capability is earned by a synthetic layout. |
| Instrument-pouch webbing threading | Front/back routing, exact entry/exit slots, marked-face orientation and twists hidden by crossings. | Identify a specific misthread or twist and propose the shortest reversible unthread/rethread sequence with the required slot and face. | An unseen rear path remains unknown until inspected. A correctly drawn path is not evidence of load rating or retention under force. This profile is non-loadbearing. |
| Compliant gasket seating | Local deformation, progressive seating, migration and the relation between a visible bulge and nearby seated regions. | Select the specific quadrant or segment to release/reseat and name the expected visual change, rather than recommending unspecified extra pressure. | Matching gasket/channel identity and permitted manipulation are confirmed inputs. A flat-looking exposed edge does not establish underside contact, compression, insertion force or leak integrity. |
| Gravity-fed water manifold | Labelled connection graph, relative heights, observed flow progression and multiple competing causes of a blocked branch. | Name the exact connection, support or reservoir-height change to test, then request an observable outcome or an actual timed-volume reading. | A still cannot establish flow direction/rate, pressure or absence of leaks. Use water-only gravity-fed benchtop configurations and preserve uncertainty about hidden ports and trapped air. |

These adaptations should be tied to the captured scene through referenced observations, a concrete next action, an expected visible result and a stop/retake condition. A fixed list of stages alone does not establish that the model adapted a physical method.

## Product and evidence gates

All five profiles need explicit operator resources: available hands, permitted tools/supports and any body-bracing restrictions. New free-text camera guidance does not inherit the older jig checker's symbolic hand-occupancy or support-transfer guarantees. The operator must review whether the proposed action can be performed with those resources.

The camera preview is continuous locally. Model inspections use captured frames. This is camera-led turn-taking; it does not establish realtime object tracking, continuous video understanding or a physically registered AR overlay. Regions belong on the assessed frozen frame. A previous camera position or object arrangement cannot authorize a later action merely because a box still looks plausible on screen.

The first view establishes a reference, rather than proving scene continuity. Later before/after comparisons need trustworthy frame and session binding. Unknown continuity, occlusion and stale evidence should request a new view. A scene or target change must invalidate dependent confirmation rather than preserving a completed-looking checklist.

Progress must require an explicit operator assessment tied to the correct current-step evidence. A model response, a clear image, a passing schema or a box marked `ready_for_review` cannot independently establish physical completion. Measurements remain operator inputs. Source uploads with unknown capture time must not be relabelled as freshly captured camera evidence.

An initial pure-engine review reproduced a quantitative-result gap: the gravity profile could finish as `operator_reviewed_complete` while its notes reported 1 mL collected against a 10 mL minimum. Its checkbox asserted that the result had been *checked*, which could truthfully include a failed result. The correction adds structured operator readings and deterministic comparisons. An independent rerun now rejects completion for 1 mL against 10 mL even with every checkbox selected. Saving that reading as incomplete is recorded as a **failed** review, with the actual value, bound and fact snapshot retained; the phase stays active and a new before/after cycle is required. This enforces the declared numerical comparison, not the truth of a person's reading or an automated flow measurement.

The same review found a missing-resource gate in the gasket's first phase: guidance could become actionable without the operator-capability fact. The catalog was corrected. A direct rerun now blocks that case, and all 20 phases require their affirmative scope and operator/handling-resource facts. This input gate does not amount to a symbolic proof that arbitrary generated prose uses only those resources.

## What can credibly be demonstrated now

Software checks can establish session ownership, state transitions, evidence binding, bounded requests, schema handling, camera lifecycle, private frame retrieval and model/API integration. Synthetic images can test whether the system produces a valid response and preserves uncertainty. They do not demonstrate a hand manipulating real material, successful recovery from real deformation, actual flow, or independent operation.

Actual participant-account requests support the model integration claim only to the extent recorded in their input/output/usage traces. Report their observed latency and errors. Do not turn a timeout budget into a measured latency or call five synthetic diagrams a benchmark of real physical reasoning. Completed execution evidence belongs in the corresponding verification record.

To establish physical usefulness, each selected profile needs one actual admitted setup, its measured/confirmed facts, a complete baseline, a camera-guided attempt, all interventions and a consequential correction. Compare against an ordinary labelled diagram/checklist or sensible support arrangement, and against an equally informed direct assistant. Give each method equivalent inputs and supplies. Include capture/review/setup effort in the comparison.

A frozen held-out change should alter a relationship: a different obstructed branch, an ambiguous fragment edge, a hidden-side misthread, a migrating gasket segment or a manifold height/connection fault. A changed title, larger object or additional checklist does not by itself establish adaptation.

## Hackathon interpretation

The supplied event text describes Indie judging as creativity, technical ambition and whether the project works. Startup judging emphasizes technical execution and production operation. “Push the Frontier” includes understanding or controlling physical systems. This direction is thematically relevant, but thematic relevance does not establish a successful physical result.

The equal weighting in that text concerns **application selection**, not a published final judging-weight table. The supplied text does not establish a requirement to use every Meta model, the complete participant Official Rules or the final submission format. Those must remain separate from product recommendations.

The defensible claim is five implemented camera-guidance profiles with separately reported software/API evidence and unresolved physical admission. A stronger claim requires the real trials and comparisons above.
