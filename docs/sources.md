## Prior art that constrains novelty

- [Bagging Device for One Handed Users, ASME 2013](https://breakme.ku.edu/2013_Bagging_Buddy.pdf): one-handed packing, observed jobs and recycled-cardboard/binderclip jigs; explicitly identifies difficulty putting an empty bag into a clamp with one hand.
- [JigFab, CHI 2019](https://www.krisluyten.net/publications/dblp_conf_chi_leenvlr19/): computational task-specific jigs, physical constraints and fabrication instructions.
- [VLMgineer, ICLR 2026](https://vlmgineer.github.io/): joint tool/action design with iterative evaluation, substantially simulation-based search and real-world examples.
- [Tandem, CHI 2024](https://depts.washington.edu/machines/projects/tandem/): physical fabrication workflows represented as multimodal programs with explicit physical/digital assertions.
- [UW CREATE adaptive device design](https://create.uw.edu/initiatives/rerc-home/device-design/): ongoing AI-assisted personalised device-design work from scans, conversation and feedback.

These sources defeat broad claims that AI-designed jigs, one-handed packing, physical feedback loops or generated instructions are themselves new. CounterShot must earn its narrower contribution through coordinated whole-job adaptation and actual comparative evidence.
