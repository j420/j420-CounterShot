# Current product focus

CounterShot's primary workflow is **custom workshop jig design**. The flagship is temporary holding/alignment/stabilisation during low-force assembly of a supplied six-panel instrument enclosure.

The jig and operating sequence are co-designed around fixed product parts, measured joint windows and operator capabilities. The current library composes a base, split exterior braces and a removable/notched front gate. It checks support transfer, joining order, access and extraction/reset.

See the [build brief](workshop-build-brief.md) for the exact physical case, measurements, admission tests and comparisons. Earlier fulfilment examples remain historical software-regression fixtures; they are not the current demonstration focus.

Candidate later workshop domains include cable-harness workholding, layered-part alignment and other manual assembly. Each needs its own part/joint representation, fixture primitives and physical admission; a discussion by the model does not make it supported.
