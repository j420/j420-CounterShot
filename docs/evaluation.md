# Comparative and held-out evaluation

The proposed contribution is bounded adaptation of observed physical work. Existing one-handed aids, template generators and multimodal assistants are strong alternatives. A successful drawing or attractive interface does not establish an advantage.

## Compare fairly

Use the same operator, stated abilities, supplies, measurements and completion criteria across conditions. Record practice differences and vary condition order when practical.

1. **Sensible low-cost aid:** try an appropriate pre-creased wrapper, ordinary holder or other available aid. Do not manufacture a problem by withholding an obvious cheap solution.
2. **Fixed parameterised template:** use the same supported package families and dimensions with a fixed arrangement/sequence. This comparison is needed for a claim beyond template selection or resizing.
3. **Direct Muse assistant:** provide the same media, facts, available components, drawing tools, factual physical feedback and comparable candidate/revision opportunity. Archive its prompts and outputs. Do not compare CounterShot's iterative tool use with an intentionally restricted one-shot chat.

Before evaluation, state model/candidate budget, designs physically built, revision opportunities, elapsed budget and permitted engineering help. Report real usage and human construction effort. If the baseline cannot complete independently, report that outcome instead of inventing a speed comparison.

## Freeze the held-out test

Hash or record a source commit plus prompts, schemas, component grammar, checker, tested material envelope, supported package subtypes, success definition and budgets. An independent reviewer selects the actual combination afterward, within that declared scope.

The strongest bounded change alters the holding requirements: for example, an admitted wraparound job becomes a measured supported tuck-carton job with an unseen item/constraint combination. A mere size change is weak evidence of structural adaptation.

After freeze, factual measurements and feedback such as “this edge catches here” are allowed. Developer-supplied support geometry or operation solutions are interventions. Preserve all attempts. A defect fix creates a new manifest and requires a new held-out case; the earlier case becomes development evidence.

## Evidence required

- The prior method's specific failure or violated constraint.
- A real change in package/insert geometry.
- A changed support relationship or station arrangement.
- A changed operation dependency or order.
- Compatible geometry, loading and release across those changes.
- Complete physical operation without undisclosed engineering repair.
- All failed and incomplete attempts, not just a selected successful clip.

Show before/after parts and the corresponding operation rather than inventing a novelty score. Component libraries are disclosed. This test can support **bounded composition and adaptation**; it does not establish arbitrary mechanical invention.

## Outcome record

For each condition, record complete cycles, total attempts, help/alternate-hand use, errors, incomplete evidence, one-time design/build time, operating time, adaptation time and material use. Results belong to the exact physical revision. A replay may be used for presentation recovery only when visibly labelled as recorded.

An internal model critic is a consistency check. Independent review and human inspection of physical evidence are separate. Review objections should distinguish a design correction from a claim still awaiting empirical testing.

## Claims after the test

Claim only the observed capability. If the methods perform equally, report any measured convenience or reproducibility benefit without inventing a novelty or superiority claim. Do not claim world-first status, universal accessibility, shipment protection or clinical benefit from this small demonstration.

The contest rewards impact, creative Meta tooling, execution and demo quality. The compelling submission shows an actual action conflict, a coordinated generated redesign, repeated complete operation and a successful consequential change. Competitive success remains uncertain.
